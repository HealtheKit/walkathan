TEF Walkathon: User and Administrator Guide

How participants, group owners and walkathon administrators use the Tresundios TEF Walkathon app on Android and iPhone.

Version 1.0 · November 2026 · All screenshots show sample names and numbers.

About the app

The app counts each walker's steps from their own phone and ranks everyone in the walkathon events they are part of.

  • Steps are counted automatically from the phone and from Apple Health or Google Health Connect. Nobody can type steps in.
  • One login, the whole family. A single email login can hold up to 6 walkers: the account holder, a spouse, children and one other person. Each walker has their own steps, on their own phone.
  • Events are walkathons with a start and end date, created by the administrators. Only invited participants take part.
  • Leaderboards rank walkers overall, and separately for men and women, by total steps, daily average, distance or active days.
  • Friends and groups let people compete with colleagues, teams or neighbours, privately.
  • Privacy first. Steps are private unless the walker is in an event, a group, or has accepted a friend.

First sign-in

There is no public sign-up. The walkathon administrator creates your login and gives you a temporary password.

  1. Install the app from the link your administrator sends (Google Play internal test or Apple TestFlight).
  2. Open it and sign in with your email and the temporary password you received.
  3. Choose your own password (at least 6 characters) and type it twice.
  4. Read How your steps are shared, tick I understand and agree, then tap Continue.
New phone?The first phone you sign in on is approved automatically, and so is any phone you have used before. A new phone shows Waiting for approval until a Super Admin or an organizer of your event approves it, then continues by itself. Until then it cannot count steps. A phone for another family member signing in with the same email is also a new phone. Super Admins can sign in from any phone without approval.
Choose your profile.Every time you sign in, the app lists your login's profiles and you pick who is walking on this phone (even if there is only one). Reopening the app without signing out does not ask again. Steps from the phone count for that profile.
Forgot your password?Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen to get a reset email, or ask your administrator for a new temporary password.

Family profiles

One login can hold up to 6 walkers. Each walker uses their own phone.

  1. Open the account menu (top-right person icon) and choose Profiles.
  2. Tap Add a profile, choose who it is (spouse, child or other), enter a name, the daily step goal and sex.
  3. Sex is needed for the men's and women's boards. Height, weight and birth year are optional and only improve calorie estimates.
  4. On each family member's own phone, sign in with the same email and tap the name chip at the top of the screen to choose who is walking on that phone.
One phone belongs to one walker.The first profile that walks with a phone owns it. Steps from that phone never count for anyone else, so family members cannot share or copy each other's steps. A mistaken choice can be changed within the first hour; otherwise a phone can move to another profile after 7 days without use.

Getting a new phone

Move your old profile's steps to the profile on your new phone. An organizer must approve it.

  1. Sign in on the new phone with the same email and make sure a profile exists for it (see Family profiles).
  2. Open Profiles and tap Got a new phone? Move a profile.
  3. Choose the profile on this phone (kept) and the old phone's profile (removed), then give a short reason.
  4. Read the warning, tick the box and tap Request approval. You can cancel the request until it is decided.
  5. When the Super Admin or an organizer of your event approves, the old profile's steps, rest days and missing details move into the new profile and the old profile is deleted. If it is rejected, nothing changes.
This cannot be undone.The old profile is permanently removed once approved. Its friends and group memberships are not carried over. Nothing happens before approval.

Home

Home answers three questions at a glance:

  • How am I doing today? Steps so far against your goal. Tap the card to open Walk.
  • Where do I stand? Your current event, the day of the event, your rank and your total.
  • Is my streak alive? Consecutive days you reached your goal. The flame grows while the streak lasts.

The tabs at the bottom are Home, Walk, Activity, Event and Social. On tablets they appear down the left side.

Walk: today's steps

  1. The first time, tap Allow so the app can read steps. On Android it may ask you to install or open Health Connect; on iPhone, allow Apple Health.
  2. Walk. The number goes up with every step while the app is open and a green Live marker shows (on iPhone it updates every couple of seconds). If the number drops slightly after a sync, steps from a cycling or machine session were removed. Your steps are uploaded about every 20 seconds while walking, and in the background on Android.
  3. When you reach your goal, the ring completes and a short celebration plays.
Offline?Steps are saved on the phone and uploaded automatically when you are back online. The screen says "Saved on this phone".

What counts as a step

Only real walking and running on the move counts.

SituationCounted?
Walking or runningYes
Steps typed in by hand in a health appNo, never
Cycling, indoor or outdoor, and exercise bikesNo
Treadmill, elliptical, rowing and stair machinesNo
Standing stillNo (the phone's own step sensor ignores it)
More than 100,000 steps in a day, or faster than humanly possibleRejected automatically
Good to knowExercise sessions are removed when your watch or fitness app records them as such. Steps are also ignored while your phone stays in one place, for example if it is shaken while you stand still, so allow location when asked. This only applies where the phone has a clear GPS signal; indoors or with a weak signal, steps count as usual. Organizers can cap or exclude an unusual day, and every change is recorded with a reason.

Activity, streaks and rest days

  • Switch between Day, Week, Month and Year; use the arrows to go back in time. Green bars reached the goal.
  • The calendar marks goal days, days with some steps and rest days. Tap any day for its details.
  • Streak: consecutive goal days. A rest day keeps your streak alive without walking. Tap today or a future day and choose Make this a rest day (up to 2 per week).
  • Monthly and yearly recaps, personal records and achievements are further down.

The event board

  • The top shows the event name, the day of the event and how much of it has passed. If you are in several events, tap the name to switch.
  • Walkers, steps together and your rank, then your standing: your percentile (for example "top 11%"), how far you are behind the next place, and how you compare with the event average.
  • Choose Event, This week or Today; Everyone, Men or Women; and rank by Total steps, Daily average, Distance or Active days.
  • Your other family profiles appear under Your household when they are further down the board.
  • Announcements from the organizers appear at the top.

Friends

  1. Open Social → Friends. Share your code (copy button) with a friend.
  2. Type a friend's code and tap Send request. They must accept before either of you sees the other's steps.
  3. Compare steps Today, This week or This month.

From a friend's menu you can Hide them (they stay friends but leave your lists), Remove them, or Block them (the friendship ends and they cannot find you again; they are not told).

Groups and challenges

Groups are private step competitions for a team, family or class, from a few people up to 15,000.

Join or create

  • Create group: give it a name. You become its owner.
  • Join with code: enter the invite code you were given. Joining shares your name and step totals with the group.

For group owners and admins

Tap the gear icon, or long-press the group name, to open Group settings:

  • Invite people: tap New invite code, then Copy invitation and paste it into a text or email. Codes last 14 days; Revoke all stops every code.
  • Mystery Mode: today's step counts are hidden and only ranks show; counts are revealed the next day.
  • Fixed-date challenge: add start and end dates (1 day to 1 year). Its board stays available after it ends until you clear it.
  • Report: copy totals per member for any dates as a spreadsheet file. Use one group per team to compare teams.
  • Members: make someone an admin, hand over ownership, or remove a member.

Settings, privacy and themes

Open the account menu and choose Settings & help.

  • Appearance: choose TEF classic (blue and gold) or Kuwait (flag green, Gulf turquoise and desert gold). Dark mode follows the phone.
  • Privacy: explains exactly who can see your steps. Each profile has a Share steps with friends switch.
  • Help & Feedback: answers to common questions, the support email, and a form to send feedback (it never includes your steps).
  • Delete account: removes your login, every profile on it and all step history, permanently. You will be asked for your password.

Administrator guide Staff only

Staff open the account menu and choose Admin dashboard. The Super Admin also sees Members (admin) and the staff-roles icon. Every permission is enforced on the server, not just hidden in the app.

Typical event, start to finish 1. Create the event as a draft and set its rules. 2. Import the participant list. 3. Share sign-in details. 4. Set the event to Live. 5. Watch the dashboard daily. 6. At the end, export the report and record rewards.

Adding members

  1. Account menu → Members (admin) → Add member.
  2. Enter the email and name. A temporary password is shown once. Copy it and send it to the person privately.
  3. From a member's menu: Reset password (new temporary password), Disable or Enable, or Delete (permanent).
Adding many people?Use the event import instead. It creates the logins and adds them to the event in one go.

Creating and running an event

  1. Admin dashboard → New event: enter a name and choose the dates (up to one year). New events start as Draft, hidden from participants.
  2. Open the event → Settings:
    • Daily target: the goal used for "target met" days.
    • Daily cap (optional): steps above this per day do not count.
    • Men's and women's boards: on or off.
    • Recognition: minimum total steps and/or minimum % of active days to qualify.
  3. Import participants (next section).
  4. Set the status to Live. Paused keeps the event visible with a notice (steps are still saved); Cancelled ends it.

In the participant list, each person's menu offers Block or Reinstate, Move to another event, and Remove.

Importing participants from a spreadsheet

  1. Prepare a list with one person per line: email or email,name. A header line is fine. Commas, semicolons and tabs all work.
  2. Open the event → Import CSV. Copy the list from your spreadsheet, then tap Paste.
  3. Tap Check list. It shows how many emails are valid, duplicates ignored, and which lines will be skipped.
  4. Tap Import. Large lists import in batches with a progress bar.
  5. New people get a login with a temporary password; people who already have a login are simply added. Tap Copy all as CSV to save the sign-in details.

Passwords are shown only once.They are not stored anywhere. Copy them before leaving the screen and share each one privately. If one is lost, use Reset password in Members. Importing the same list again is safe.

Event dashboard

Open an event → Dashboard.

  • Health: participants, how many have walked, active today, total steps and distance, % synced in the last 48 hours, how many qualify for recognition, and the last leaderboard update.
  • Participation donut and daily participation chart.
  • Highlights: top walkers, most consistent, most improved over 7 days.
  • Needs attention: filter by No step data, Dropout risk, Not synced in 48 h, Unusually high day or Low consistency. Tap a person to review them.
  • The table icon at the top copies a full report for spreadsheets: rank, name, email, sex, totals, active days, consistency, eligibility, reward and flags.

Reviewing step data

Each participant's page shows their totals and every day's steps, with where they came from (Health app or phone sensor).

  • Correct a day (verifiers): tap the pencil next to a day and choose Exclude this day or Cap this day. A reason is required and is kept in the audit trail. Remove correction undoes it.
  • Request re-sync (verifiers): the person's phone uploads its last month again on its next sync.
  • Set sex, Set reward, Block from event (organizers).
Participants can never change their own steps.Corrections are only for staff, and every one is logged.

Announcements and rewards

  • Announce (event page): write a title and message and send it to All, Men or Women. It appears at the top of the participants' Event tab. Push notifications are not enabled.
  • Rewards: on a participant's page, tap Set reward, enter the award (for example "Gold medal") and its status: selected, awarded or delivered. The participant sees it under Recognition.
  • Eligibility is calculated from the event's recognition rules and shown on the dashboard and in the report.

Approving profile changes and new phones

  1. Super Admins and event organizers see Approvals in the account menu, with a count of waiting requests.
  2. Each request shows the member's email, the old and new profile totals (steps and days), when the old phone last uploaded, and the member's reason.
  3. Tap Approve to merge and delete the old profile, or Reject to leave everything as it is. Add a note if useful. Check that the reason is genuine, because approval is permanent.
  4. The New phones tab lists phones waiting to be approved. It shows the member's email, the phone, and how many other approved phones they have. Approve only if you are sure it is their own phone. Reject lets them see your note and ask again.
  5. You only see members of events you organize. You cannot approve your own request; another organizer or the Super Admin must. The History tab lists past decisions, and every decision is recorded.

Checking and resetting counts

  • Where a total comes from. Open a participant. Each day lists every reading (Health app and phone sensor), the day total, and what actually counted after corrections, caps and resets. The Health app reading wins when both exist; they are never added together.
  • Verify all counts (count-tools icon on the event dashboard, or the button on the event page). It recounts the event from the raw readings and checks that daily totals, weekly totals, the participant list and the leaderboard agree. Any mismatch is listed; Repair totals rebuilds the totals from the raw readings.
  • Set to zero. Open the event and turn on the Set all profiles to zero switch to reset everyone in that event; turn it off to bring the counts back. To reset one person, open them from the dashboard and tap Set to zero. Super Admins and the event's organizers can do this. A reason is required and recorded. Steps walked before the reset stop counting, even if a phone uploads them later, and new steps count from that moment. Undo reset brings the counts back.
A reset is a fresh start.Each walker's own count for the day of the reset also restarts from zero on their phone the next time it syncs, so the Walk screen and the boards agree. Earlier days in their history are kept. Undoing the reset brings that day back.

Event organizers

An event can have several organizers. The Super Admin opens an event, finds the Organizers card, taps Add and enters the person's login email (they must already be a member). Use the cross to remove one. Organizers only approve changes for members of the events they are added to. An organizer's login must have only one profile: a login with several family profiles can't be made an organizer, and an organizer can't add more profiles.

Staff roles

  1. The Super Admin opens the Admin dashboard and taps the staff-roles icon at the top.
  2. Enter the person's email (they must already be a member), choose Organizer, Verifier or Viewer, and tap Grant role.
  3. Use the remove icon to take a role away.

Troubleshooting

My steps are not updating

Open the app once; check the Walk screen for an Allow or Health Connect message; make sure you are online. If the screen says "Saved on this phone", it will upload automatically. Some phones' battery savers delay background updates; opening the app syncs immediately. If the number does not move while you walk, allow Physical activity (Android) or Motion & Fitness (iPhone) for the app in your phone's settings. On Android, the total can also catch up late after you reopen the app, because Health Connect is updated by apps such as Google Fit or Samsung Health, sometimes 15 minutes or more behind.

"This phone counts steps for someone else"

Each phone belongs to one walker. Switch back to that person with the name chip, or, within an hour of first use, tap Use this phone for … instead. Otherwise the phone can switch after 7 days without use.

I changed phones and my old steps are missing

Use Profiles → Got a new phone? Move a profile and wait for an organizer to approve. Until then the old steps stay on the old profile.

Some of my steps disappeared

Steps recorded during cycling, treadmill or exercise-machine sessions are removed, and the Walk screen tells you how many. An organizer may also have capped an unusual day.

I am not on the men's or women's board

Add your sex in Profiles. You are always on the overall board.

I cannot see the event

Events appear once the organizers add you and set the event to Live. Ask them to check the participant list.

A group invite code does not work

Codes expire after 14 days and can be revoked or used up. Ask the group owner for a new code. After several wrong tries, wait 15 minutes.

I forgot my password

Use Forgot password? on the sign-in screen, or ask an administrator to reset it.

Questions and answers

Does the app use GPS or my location?

Yes, but only to check that you are really moving, so shaking the phone while standing still does not add steps. Location is processed on your phone, is never uploaded, and nobody can see where you walk. On Android a small "Counting your walk" notification shows while it runs. If you decline, the app still counts steps but cannot ignore shaking.

Who can see my steps?

Only you, unless you are in an event (other participants see your name and totals), a group you joined, or you accepted a friend and have sharing on.

Is my health data used for advertising?

No. Step and health data is never used for ads or shared with advertisers.

Can my child have a profile?

Yes, as one of the up to 6 profiles on your login. Please add a child only if you are their parent or guardian; their name and steps can appear on event boards.

What happens when I delete my account?

Your login, every profile on it and all step history are deleted permanently.
